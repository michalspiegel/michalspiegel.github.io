Hello, I’m

Michal Spiegel

Masaryk University · ML Engineer at Filevine

I’m a master’s student at Masaryk University, interested in machine learning and interpretability. Right now I spend a lot of time thinking about positional encodings in Transformers — especially what small models can actually learn with RoPE, and how they pull it off.

Alongside research, I work full-time as an ML Engineer at Filevine, building retrieval and question-answering systems for very large collections of legal documents. In short: lots of embeddings, lots of evaluation, and an unreasonable number of PDFs.

I also enjoy taking research on the road. In 2025 I presented our work at EMNLP in Suzhou and attended ACL in Vienna. In March 2026, I spent a month in Japan with LLMc at the National Institute of Informatics, benchmarking how robust language models are on olympiad math problems.
